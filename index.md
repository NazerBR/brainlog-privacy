# BrainLog

BrainLog is a private, single-user assistant operated by its owner for their own notes,
tasks and reminders. It is not offered to the public and has no sign-up.

With the owner's consent, it reads the owner's Gmail messages and Google Calendar events
(read-only) to suggest tasks and reminders in the owner's private Telegram chat.
It cannot send email or change calendar events.
